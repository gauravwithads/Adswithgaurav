1.Introduction

AdsWithGaurav ("we", "us", "our") is a Meta Ads agency based in Faridabad, Haryana, India. We run Facebook and Instagram advertising campaigns for local businesses.

This Privacy Policy applies to the website at gauravwithads.github.io/Adswithgaurav and to the information you share with us through the website, WhatsApp, phone or email. By using this website or submitting your details, you agree to the practices described here.

2.Information we collect

Information you give us

When you submit the "Request Free Audit" form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your business type
  • Your city
  • Your phone or WhatsApp number

If you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email, we also keep the details you share in those conversations, such as your business information and advertising goals.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider and the third-party services used on this site may automatically receive basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages you visit. We do not use this information to identify you personally.

3.How we use your information

We process your information based on your consent, which you give when you submit the form or contact us. We use it to:

  • Contact you about your free audit or strategy call
  • Understand your business and recommend suitable advertising plans
  • Respond to your questions and requests
  • Deliver and manage our services if you become a client
  • Keep records and meet legal or tax obligations where required

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to send unrelated promotional messages.

4.Where your data is stored

Form submissions are sent through Google Apps Script to a private Google Sheet and are stored on Google's servers. Access to this sheet is restricted to us. Because Google operates globally, your information may be processed on servers located outside India.

Conversations on WhatsApp are handled by WhatsApp (Meta) and are subject to their own terms and privacy policy.

5.Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only in these limited cases:

  • Service providers: companies that help us run this website and our business, such as Google and WhatsApp, only as needed to provide their service.
  • Legal requirements: where disclosure is required by law, court order or a valid request from a government authority.
  • Protection of rights: where necessary to protect our rights, safety or property, or those of others.

7.Data retention

We keep your information only for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry, provide our services, and meet legal or accounting requirements. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous. You may also request deletion at any time (see Section 9).

8.Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including restricting access to our data records and using trusted providers. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9.Your rights and choices

You have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • Ask us to delete your personal information
  • Withdraw your consent to our use of your information at any time
  • Raise a complaint or grievance about how your information is handled

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 13. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

10.Children's privacy

Our services are intended for business owners and are not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us their information, please contact us and we will delete it.
